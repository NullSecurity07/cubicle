## Riskiest guesses

1. **Unique value proposition (Canva for indie board games)**: Risky because designers may view templates as a one-time project need rather than a recurring €39/month subscription tool, or may prefer existing tools like Canva or InDesign. Settled by: Confirming that active designers are willing to pay a recurring monthly fee for print-and-play templates and feedback sorting across multiple projects.
2. **Channels (Indie board-game communities, Kickstarter pre-launch networks, conventions)**: Risky because these communities often have strict rules against self-promotion and organic acquisition channels may yield very low conversion without paid ads (which are currently restricted by decision). Settled by: Tracking signups from specific community posts and outreach.
3. **Cost structure**: Risky because customer acquisition costs and hosting/support overhead for design templates and data collection could scale faster than €39/month subscription revenue. Settled by: Tracking actual time and server costs per active user.
