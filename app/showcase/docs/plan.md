# Launch plan

One launch at a time. The team plans, drafts and checks; the founder posts every item, one at a time.

## The product in one sentence

Kindling is a print-and-play kit for indie board-game designers providing true-printing templates and a playtest feedback sorter.

## Who it is for

Solo and two-person indie board-game designers preparing a prototype for playtests, conventions, or a Kickstarter.
