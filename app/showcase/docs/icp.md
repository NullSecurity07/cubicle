## Who

What I sell (one line, plain English): Kindling is a print-and-play kit for indie board-game designers: card, board and token templates that print true on a home printer, a rules-sheet layout, and a playtest feedback form that sorts what testers say. (the founder said)

Who buys it (title, company size, industry): Solo and two-person indie board-game designers preparing a prototype for playtests, conventions or a Kickstarter. (the founder said)

Who influences the decision (champion vs signer): The designer acts as both champion and signer. (the founder said)
