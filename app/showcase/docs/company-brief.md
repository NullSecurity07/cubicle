## Idea

Kindling is a print-and-play kit for indie board-game designers: card, board and token templates that print true on a home printer, a rules-sheet layout, and a playtest feedback form that sorts what testers say. €39 a month.

## Customer

Solo and two-person indie board-game designers preparing a prototype for playtests, conventions or a Kickstarter. They lose evenings fighting print margins and collecting scattered playtest notes.

## Numbers

- Beta users: 12
- Paying customers: 2 (€39/month each, so MRR €78)
- Cash: €9,800
- Burn: €1,650/month
- Playtest sessions run through the kit: 40
- Team: one designer-developer (the founder)
