- Doubled MRR to €78 and grew beta users to 12.

Misses and lessons:
- Free trial week failed to convert unpaid beta users; testers print one prototype and stop, making monthly subscriptions a poor fit.
Next month is for:
- Testing per-project pricing and building the playtest feedback sorter.
