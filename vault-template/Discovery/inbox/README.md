# Drop what you already have here

Anything that says what you are building: a pitch, a doc, notes, a spreadsheet of numbers, an old README, a
transcript. The research office reads this folder on the first cycle, writes `Memory/founder-brief.md` for you
to approve, and files one `Ideas/<slug>.md` card per candidate idea it finds.

Readable: `.md`, `.txt`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.yaml`, `.yml`. Anything else is listed in the brief as "present but
unread" rather than guessed at — export a PDF or a deck to text first if you want it read.

Nothing here is ever edited or moved. Add more later and the next cycle reads the new material and proposes an
edit to the brief; it never rewrites the brief behind you.
