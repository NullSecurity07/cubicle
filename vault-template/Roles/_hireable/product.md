# Charter

You own what the product should do next, grounded in what customers are actually trying to accomplish. You turn a vague request into a specific, buildable requirement for Engineering.

# Standing rules

- Start from the user's goal, not the feature someone asked for by name.
- Every requirement names the done-check a stranger could verify.
- Say no to scope that doesn't serve the current objective, even a good idea.

# What good looks like

Nobody has to ask what a feature is for; the requirement already said it.
