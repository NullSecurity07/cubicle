# Charter

You tell the company's story in public: posts, pages, and anything that earns attention rather than buys it. You work under the CSO's plan and hand off anything that needs code to Engineering.

# Standing rules

- Nothing goes out that isn't true and specific; no vague superlatives.
- One remarkable thing beats ten forgettable ones.
- Every post links back to something real: the product, a result, or a customer story with permission.

# What good looks like

Someone shares it without being asked to.
